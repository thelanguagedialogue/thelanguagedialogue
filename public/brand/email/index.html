  1. Open outlook.com on your computer and click the gear (Settings) at the top right.
  2. Go to Account → Signatures (in the new Outlook: Accounts → Signatures).
  3. Click + New signature, name it "The Language Dialogue", click in the big box and paste (Cmd + V, or Ctrl + V on Windows).
  4. Click Save. Below it, under Default signatures, choose it for new messages and for replies/forwards, then Save again.

The Outlook app on the computer picks it up by itself. The Outlook app on the phone only allows plain text, so there you can type just your name and the website.

Your plan-and-prices email

The website promises "after the free call you get your price in writing". This is that email. Save it once as a template; each time you only fill in the [brackets].

Subject line (type it in the subject field, a template keeps only the text):
Your [Italian / Spanish / French] lessons: plan and prices

Hi [first name],

It was lovely to talk with you today. Here is what we said, so you have it in writing.

Your goal: [in their words, e.g. talk with your in-laws in Italian by next summer]
Your level: [Beginner / Intermediate / Advanced / Business]
Format: [one-on-one / small group], [in person in (area) / online]
What I suggest: [e.g. 2 hours a week, two lessons of 1 hour]

Prices
Single lesson, 60 minutes: $[ ]
Package of 10 lessons: $[ ] ($[ ] per lesson)
Package of 20 lessons: $[ ] ($[ ] per lesson)
Small group, per person, 60 minutes: $[ ]

[Optional offer, e.g. "Book a package before (date) and your first lesson is 30 minutes longer at no cost."]

How it works: we book lessons week by week, and you can move a lesson if you tell me [24] hours before.
Payment: [Zelle / Venmo / card], [before the first lesson of each package].

If it sounds good, just reply with the days and times that work for you and we start.

  1. In outlook.com, click New mail.
  2. In the toolbar of the new email, click Apps (the icon with four squares; it can also sit under ⋯) and choose My Templates.
  3. Click + Template, call it "After the free call", paste the email into the big box and click Save.
  4. Next time: New mail → Apps → My Templates → click "After the free call", write the subject line, then fill in the brackets. Your signature is added below by itself.

Two tips: write their goal with their own words from the call, it makes the email feel written for them. And keep 3 or 4 price lines at most, more choices slow people down.
