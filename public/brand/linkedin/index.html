Your LinkedIn kit

Everything for your LinkedIn profile, in the order you paste it: a cover image in your colours, your headline, your About, The Language Dialogue as your new position, your services and one short post. About 10 minutes, on the computer or in the app.

Your current jobs stay as they are. You only add, nothing gets deleted.

1 · cover image

The picture at the top

Download the image
  1. Open your profile and click the camera / pencil on the grey area at the top (the background photo).
  2. Choose Upload photo, pick the image you downloaded, then Apply.

The left side is empty on purpose: your own photo sits there. On the phone, press and hold the image above and choose Save to Photos.

2 · headline

The line under your name

Italian & Spanish teacher in Atlanta and online · French for beginners · Founder, The Language Dialogue
  1. Click the pencil next to your name.
  2. Paste it in Headline and click Save.

Your website on the profile

  2. Add website: paste the link, type Business (or Personal), then Save.

4 · about

Your About

I'm Valentina, a native Italian. I teach Italian and Spanish at all levels and French for beginners, to adults, teens and kids, in the Atlanta metro area and online. For seventeen years I worked for an Italian multinational fashion company, training and coaching store teams across Europe, the Middle East and Russia, in more than 300 stores. Nobody learns to run a shop floor from a textbook, and nobody learns to speak from one either. You learn by doing it, with someone who corrects you kindly and keeps you talking. That is how I teach. We talk from the first lesson, and grammar comes in when it helps you say what you want to say. Every student starts with a reason: a trip, a family, a job, the Italian citizenship test, or just the pleasure of it. I build the lessons around it. I studied foreign languages and communication at the University of Turin, English at the University of Glamorgan in Wales, and Spanish at Proyecto Español in Alicante. Today I also teach for four language schools. The first call is free, 15 minutes: thelanguagedialogue.com
  1. Scroll to About and click its pencil. No About yet? Click Add profile section → Add about.
  2. Paste it and click Save.

6 · services

Your services

This makes you show up when people on LinkedIn look for a teacher.

  1. Under your name click Open to → Providing services.
  2. Type tutor and language in the services box and pick the ones that fit.
  3. Location: Atlanta, and say yes to working remotely (that covers online lessons).
