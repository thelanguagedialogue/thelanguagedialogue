Your LinkedIn kit

Everything for your LinkedIn profile, in the order you paste it: a cover image in your colours, your headline, your About, The Language Dialogue as your new position, your services, two posts and a short note to ask for recommendations. About 15 minutes, on the computer or in the app.

Your current jobs stay as they are. You only add, nothing gets deleted.

1 · cover image

The picture at the top

Download the image
  1. Open your profile and click the camera / pencil on the grey area at the top (the background photo).
  2. Choose Upload photo, pick the image you downloaded, then Apply.

The left side is empty on purpose: your own photo sits there. On the phone, press and hold the image above and choose Save to Photos.

8 · recommendations

Let others say it

When you say you are a good teacher, it is a claim. When a student or a school says it on your profile, people believe it. Ask 3 people who are on LinkedIn: a school you teach for, a parent, an adult student.

  1. Scroll to Recommendations (or Add profile section → Recommended → Request recommendations).
  2. Pick the person, choose how you know them, and paste this in the message:
Hi [name], I just put my lessons on LinkedIn as The Language Dialogue. Would you write me a short recommendation? Two or three lines are perfect: what you wanted when we started, and how it's going now. Thank you so much! Valentina

Let them write it in their own words, even if it is short. "Before and after" in one line is worth more than a long paragraph of praise.
